# Compact hold and grip logging — 14 October 2026

Hold movements now accept attempts, seconds per attempt and optional load once. Prescribed holds offer **Done as planned**; changed or unplanned values offer **Done with these values**. **Change** opens individual attempts for different times, loads, effort ratings or missed attempts. Progression, grip style, assistance, position measurements, technique, pain and notes remain available.

The compact view saves individual attempt rows through the existing workout writer, offline queue and correction flow. Mixed times and loads stay separate. Prescribed attempts must be confirmed before finishing; edits to attempt values clear confirmation. Completed attempts need an actual positive duration. Missed attempts remain recorded but do not contribute to hold totals. Optional loads and effort ratings stay unknown when left blank, and overall session effort does not fill missing individual ratings.

Positive grip loads require an explicit type: added weight, assistance/counterweight or implement weight. Bodyweight attempts use blank or zero load. This applies to Grip exercises with either hold or grip-hold tracking. Load meaning is saved with each attempt. Timed circuit and kettlebell prescriptions retain their existing logging paths. No database migration or rewrite of saved programmes, assignments or training records was needed.

Validation covered source confirmation, shared edits and reload recovery, actual per-attempt values, load meaning, missed attempts, unknown effort, conservative mixed-time handling, workout recovery and correction, supporting practice and programme reviews. Focused component and helper tests, TypeScript, targeted lint and production build passed. A local preview of the actual compact component at 390 pixels showed no horizontal overflow and one confirmation marked the movement recorded. Published-app verification and live user-data write tests were not performed; GitHub push and Lovable publication remain user-managed.
