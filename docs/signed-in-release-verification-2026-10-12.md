## Data preservation and limits

A baseline was captured after sign-in and the initial Today load. All **56 public-table fingerprints matched exactly** at the end of the checks: **120 sessions, 207 entries, 399 sets**, with programme assignments, prescriptions and suggested plans unchanged. Browser selectors/disclosures, cached references and unsaved preview state are outside that database comparison. No training values were entered, and no save, completion, correction, deletion or programme-change action was submitted.

Starting a programme workout creates a plan, so this read-only pass verifies the offer and enabled control rather than pressing it. Live start/save, offline finish/reconnect/retry, correction and restore workflows still require a separately authorized disposable-record test, a fresh baseline, exact cleanup and restoration verification. Other programme families and histories beyond 1,000 records retain isolated/local test coverage; the current live profile is smaller.
